# Commercial licensing

Ha-Kake is free software, released under the **GNU Affero General Public
License, version 3 or later** (AGPL-3.0-or-later). The documentation is under
CC BY-SA 4.0.

**For almost everybody, that is the only license you need.** You do not have to
read any further, and you do not have to ask permission.

## You do not need a commercial license

The AGPL restricts *distribution*, not *use*. Nobody needs a separate license
to run Ha-Kake, including to run it for money. That explicitly covers:

- Individuals reading their own car.
- Hobbyists, tinkerers and the wider Leaf-hacking community.
- Researchers, students, teachers, and anyone writing about the results.
- **Independent repair shops, mobile mechanics and EV technicians** who use
  Ha-Kake on customers' cars as part of a paid service, and charge for that
  work. This project *wants* mechanics using it. Diagnosing a pack, quoting a
  battery job off a real SOH reading, handing a customer a printout — all fine,
  no license required, no royalty, no phoning home.

You can also modify your own copy freely and keep those changes to yourself,
as long as you are not handing the modified version to anyone else.

## What the AGPL actually asks of you

Two triggers matter:

1. **If you distribute a modified version** — shipping it on a device, bundling
   it in a product, handing a customer a binary — you must give the recipients
   the complete corresponding source of your version, under the AGPL.
2. **If you offer a modified version to others over a network** — a hosted
   dashboard, a SaaS fleet-monitoring portal, a shop's customer web portal
   built on Ha-Kake — section 13 says the users interacting with it over that
   network must be offered your complete corresponding source, under the AGPL.
   This is the clause that distinguishes AGPL from plain GPL, and it is
   deliberate.

"Complete corresponding source" means the whole thing you actually run, not a
patch file and not just the parts you feel like sharing.

## You may want a commercial license

A separate, proprietary license exists for the case where the AGPL's terms do
not fit your business:

- You want to ship Ha-Kake, or code derived from it, inside a **closed-source
  product** — a scan tool, a dealer diagnostic suite, an OEM or fleet
  appliance — without publishing your source.
- You want to run a **modified version as a hosted or cloud service** for your
  customers without publishing your source under section 13.
- Your legal or procurement department will not accept copyleft in the product
  line, whatever the technical merits.

In those situations you cannot comply with the AGPL and also keep your source
closed. The maintainer owns 100% of the copyright in Ha-Kake and can therefore
grant you different terms.

Useful things to say up front: what the product or service is, roughly how many
vehicles or installations, whether you need to modify the code, and whether you
need support or just the license. A short honest description gets a faster
answer than an NDA.

## The small print about this page

This document is an **invitation to talk**, not a price list and not a binding
offer. Terms, scope and fees are agreed in writing, case by case; nothing here
creates a license or a contract. It is also not legal advice — if you are
unsure whether your situation triggers the AGPL, ask your own counsel, and
feel free to ask the maintainer too.

If you are here because you are not sure which side of the line you are on:
just write. Most questions turn out to have the answer "the AGPL already covers
you, go ahead."
